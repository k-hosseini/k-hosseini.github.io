\section{Job Market Paper}
\paper{Immigrants' Pathways to Success: Study Permit vs Permanent Residency}\\[0.3em]
Using the Longitudinal Immigration Database linked with annual tax files, I compare the long-run economic trajectories of immigrants who first arrived in Canada on study permits and later obtained permanent residency with those of immigrants admitted directly as permanent residents. Former international students earn less than direct permanent residents during the first eight years after admission, but their income trajectories surpass those of direct permanent residents in subsequent years.
